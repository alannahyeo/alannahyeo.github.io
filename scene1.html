How to Stay Safe Online

Here are some important lessons based on Eli's story

Avoid Sharing Location

Turn off location tagging and avoid posting photos that reveal where you are in real-time.

Think Before You Share

Refrain from sharing personal details like your school, schedule, or family info publicly.

Be Cautious of DMs

Never send private or inappropriate photos. Block and report anyone who makes you feel uncomfortable.

Trust Your Instincts

If something feels off—like messages from strangers or pressure to respond—trust your gut and take action.
